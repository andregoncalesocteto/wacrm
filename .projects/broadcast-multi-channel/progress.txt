- **`deliverBroadcast` (src/lib/whatsapp/broadcast-core.ts) is ALREADY mostly generic** — already uses
  `getProvider(plan.connection.channel_type)` + `getConnectionCredentials` (read once) + `provider.send(...)`
  directly (NOT `sendOutbound` — broadcast doesn't write `messages`/`conversations` per recipient like a
